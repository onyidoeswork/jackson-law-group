No lawsuit brings someone back. A wrongful death case can hold the responsible party accountable and protect the family's financial future.

We handle these cases with care for the family and no patience for the insurance company.

How These Cases Arise

  • Car, truck, and bus crashes
  • Pedestrian and bicycle knockdowns

Who Brings the Case

In New York, a wrongful death case is brought by the personal representative of the estate, on behalf of the family members who suffered the loss. If no one has been appointed yet, we handle that process in Surrogate's Court.

What the Family Can Recover

  • Loss of the financial support the person would have provided
  • Loss of the services, care, and guidance the person provided, including a parent's guidance to children
  • Medical bills related to the final injury
  • Funeral and burial expenses
  • The pain and suffering the person experienced before death, through the estate's own claim

Deadlines

A wrongful death lawsuit generally must be filed within two years of the date of death.

If a public agency is responsible, an early written notice of claim is also required. Do not wait for the estate paperwork to be finished before calling a lawyer.

Deadlines can be shorter or longer depending on the facts. Call before you assume it is too late or that you have time.

Common Questions

There is no will. Can we still bring a case?

Yes. The court can appoint an administrator for the estate. We guide the family through it.

How is the money divided?

Wrongful death proceeds go to the family members who suffered the financial loss, in proportions approved by the court.

How much does it cost to start?

Nothing up front. There is no fee unless we recover money for the family.
